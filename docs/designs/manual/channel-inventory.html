Channel Inventory: what each sales channel holds

Stock as the channels report it, and a week-by-week projection of how long it will last.

1 · Our warehouseGoods are received in Inbound Receiving and counted in Warehouse Inventory.
2 · ShipmentsStock is sent to the channels on FBA or WFS shipments (Inventory Management > Shipments).
3 · InventoryShows what Amazon (and other channels) report: available, reserved, inbound, unsellable, on hand.
4 · Channel INVPProjects stock per SKU for 12 weeks against a 30-day clearance target, using the daily sale and containers in transit.

Use Inventory to see today's channel stock. Use Channel INVP to decide what to reorder or ship next.

6Daily sales

Read Daily sale; type Potential

Daily sale: units a day over the last 30 days. Potential can be typed over. Projected = Potential × (1 + growth).

7Week columns

8Week cell

Read → total inventory, in transit, days of cover

In transit: container units that set off that week. Days: how long the stock lasts at the daily sale (∞ if nothing sells).

Channel INVP: cell colours

Each week's cell is coloured by how many days its stock lasts at the daily sale.

Colour levelRuleTypical action
Stock finishedNo stock leftCheck inbound containers and shipments; reorder or ship.
Stock near endUp to 7 daysShip or reorder now.
Clears on target8 to 20 daysHealthy: stock clears in time.
Stock high, sales moderate21 to 30 daysHold off on more stock.
Stock high, sales lowOver 30 days, or not sellingToo much stock for the sales; do not add more.

The day limits (7, 20 and 30) come from the server and are shown in the legend under the table on the screen.
