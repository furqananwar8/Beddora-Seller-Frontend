Finance: pay suppliers and vendors with approval

From partner and cost center, to a payment request, approvers, the payment process, and paid.

1 · Set upPartner Profile (who is paid) and Cost Center (expense types) must exist first.
2 · Payment RequestRaise it against an Invoice, Purchase Order, Sample or Shipment. Save draft, or Process For Approval.
3 · ApproversPeople set in Settings > Approvals are told and approve or reject, in order.
4 · Payment ProcessApproved requests appear here as PAYMENT PENDING. Finance records payments with proofs.
5 · PaidBalance reaches zero: PAID. The requester is told.

Where it starts: when a Purchase Order is approved in Procurement, the people on the Settings > Approvals Notification tab are told to raise a payment request.

Finance menu screenUse it to
Partner ProfileKeep suppliers and vendors: address, currency, payment term (sets the due date) and bank or card details.
Bank ProfileKeep the company bank accounts and cards that payments are made from.

What the other three tabs ask for.

Only approved, open POs with an amount left to pay are offered. The PO sets currency, destination and terms.
