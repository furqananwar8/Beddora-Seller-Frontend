Scanning Sessions: every scan, on record

Product UPCs are scanned in three places. Each scan session is recorded here with what matched and how inventory changed.

1 · Shipping > OrdersRow menu > Scan items: scan the product UPCs of a customer order.
2 · Inventory Management > ShipmentsScan units out of the warehouse for an FBA or WFS shipment.
3 · Inbound ReceivingScan the units of a container delivered to the warehouse.
4 · Scanning > SessionsEvery session appears here: source, channel, who, scanned, matched, not matched, units adjusted.
5 · Fix and rescanA UPC that matches nothing is not adjusted: add the UPC to the product, then scan again in a new session.

Scanning itself happens in each screen's Scan items window. This module is where you review it.
