Inventory Management: stock from warehouse to channel

Receive containers, count stock, plan and send it to Amazon FBA or Walmart WFS, and keep every SKU mapped.

1 · Inbound ReceivingA container delivered to the warehouse is counted by scanning, signed and confirmed. Good units join Our Warehouse stock.
2 · Warehouse InventoryStock per SKU in Canada, returns, damaged, 3PL, at the platforms and in transit.
3 · PlannerDecide what to send, using sales velocity and days of stock left.
4 · ShipmentsSplit reserved stock into FBA or WFS inbound shipments and track them to receipt; scan units out.
5 · ChecksStock Adjustment after a count, SKU Mapping, Inventory Aging, Seller Central.

Every scan session started from Shipments or Inbound Receiving is recorded under Scanning > Sessions.

Menu screenUse it to
Planner · ShipmentsPlan stock and send it to FBA or WFS.
Adjustments · Stock AdjustmentCorrect on-hand quantities and box dimensions · correct a warehouse count for today or a past day.
SKU MappingBind channel listings to internal SKUs.
Warehouse Inventory · SamplesStock per SKU everywhere · samples suppliers send (they never change stock).
Inbound Receiving · Inventory Aging · Seller CentralReceive containers · age of units at Amazon · shipments created on the channels.

The partner status says where the shipment is; our own status says whether units were scanned out.

FieldStatusMeaning and next step
StatusIn progressBeing prepared. Follow the next action shown on the row.
ShippedMarked as shipped; on the way to Amazon or Walmart.
Receiving · ClosedThe channel is receiving it · fully received and closed.
CancelledCancelled; it keeps the step it stopped at.
Our statusBookedConfirmed with the partner. Scan the units out when they leave the warehouse.
Scanned & shippedEvery booked unit was scanned out of the warehouse.
Next action (FBA)Send to Amazon → Enter box contents → Choose placementDraft stays internal until sent. Then pack boxes, then pick one of Amazon's placement options.
Choose delivery window → Enter carrier → Generate labels → Mark as shippedRequest the window, enter your carrier, make box, pallet and FNSKU labels, then mark shipped once the truck has left.
Next action (WFS)Send to Walmart → Enter cartons → Enter carrier → Generate labels → Mark as shippedWalmart splits the order into shipments; each gets cartons and a carrier.
