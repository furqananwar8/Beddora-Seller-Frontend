Containers: ship, track and cost what is packed

A container carries one packaging list. It is tracked from booking to goods received, and its landed cost is worked out per SKU.

1 · Packaging ListCreated against an approved PO in Procurement. Units, cartons, CBM and weight are counted there.
2 · New containerPick the PO; the list is attached and the container starts as BOOKED.
3 · ShippingUpdate ETD, ETA, bill of lading and status as it moves: on board, at port, customs, delivery.
4 · ReceivingMark it delivered to the warehouse on arrival; confirming the receipt in Inbound Receiving sets GOODS RECEIVED.
5 · CostExpenses are added; Cost Analysis and SKU Landed Cost show the landed cost per unit.

Payment requests that carry the container number show on the container, so costs from Finance line up with it.

Containers menu screenUse it to
ContainersList, filter, create and open containers; update shipping dates and status.
Cost AnalysisPer container: goods (FOB), expenses and landed total, with the SKU breakdown.
SKU Landed CostPer SKU: landed cost in CAD and USD, plus shipment cost and warehouse overhead.

Container statuses

In lifecycle order. Stages only move forward; inside shipping a container can move freely between its legs.

StatusStageMeaning and what can change
BOOKEDBookedNew container. Everything can be edited and packaging lists added or removed; it can move to any shipping status. It can be deleted while it has no list.
IN TRANSIT · ON RAIL · ON BOARDShippingIt has left. Only the ETA, the shipped-on-board date and the status can change; packaging lists are locked.
ARRIVAL AT PORT · CUSTOMS CLEARED · OUT FOR DELIVERYShippingSame limits as above. From any shipping status the container can be marked DELIVERED TO WAREHOUSE.
DELIVEREDDeliveredOlder status; nothing sets it any more. It can only be moved on to DELIVERED TO WAREHOUSE.
DELIVERED TO WAREHOUSEAt warehouseSet on the container form when it arrives; the delivery date is stamped. Waiting in Inbound Receiving; nothing else can change, documents can still be added.
GOODS RECEIVEDReceivedSet only by confirming the receipt in Inbound Receiving. Final.
NEEDS DETAILSBadgeA placeholder container waiting for its number, bill of lading and dates.
