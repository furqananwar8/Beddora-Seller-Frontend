Settings: set up who approves, who is paid from where, and who can log in

Most of what other modules depend on is configured here once: approval rules, warehouses, GST rates, company letterhead and users.

Settings screenUse it toUsed by
Approvals (admin only)Set who approves Price Analysis, Purchase Orders, Purchase Invoices and Payment Requests, in what order, and who is notified.Procurement, Finance
WarehousesKeep your own warehouse locations.PO delivery address, ship-from on shipments
AddressesShip-from addresses for FBA shipments.Inventory Management > Shipments, Shipping
GSTThe GST percentages payment requests can use.Finance > Payment Request
CompanyCompany name, address and contact printed as the letterhead.PO PDFs, order invoices
GeneralChange your own password.You
UsersInvite people and choose which features they can use.Everyone

Which Settings screens a person sees depends on their feature access. Some, such as Approvals, are admin only.

Approvals: approval rules

Who approves what, in which order. One rule per action. Staff names are blurred.

Command / function

1Tabs

Click → Approval rules, Departments, Notifications

2Rule list

Click → pick a rule

Procurement: PO Submit, Invoice Submit, Price analysis Approve. Finance: Payment request Submit. A badge shows the number of steps.

Choose → Any one approves, Everyone approves, One from each team

How a step finishes before the next one starts.

4People in the step

Pick → named people, department heads, or by platform

8"none" note

Read → no steps means all holders of the page permission are notified

There are no amount conditions and no on/off switch.
