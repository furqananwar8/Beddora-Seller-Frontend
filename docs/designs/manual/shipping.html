Shipping: book and track customer parcels

Orders from Amazon, Walmart and Temu, plus manual shipments, are rated, booked with a carrier provider and tracked.

1 · OrdersChannel orders arrive automatically. A manual shipment is made with Create shipment.
2 · Scan itemsScan the product UPCs of an order; the session is recorded under Scanning > Sessions.
3 · Get ratesCompare rates from the providers: Stallion Express, Shippo, UniUni, FedEx.
4 · BookReview and book. Fulfillment becomes Booked and the label and tracking number appear.
5 · TrackTracking status follows the carrier until Delivered.

Orders the seller already shipped with the channel's own carrier show as Shipped; only their status and tracking are displayed.

Shipping statuses and terms

Fulfillment is the order's state with us; Tracking status is the parcel's state with the carrier.

FieldStatusMeaning
FulfillmentNot ReadyThe order cannot ship yet (for example payment still pending on the channel).
To BookReady to ship: get rates and book a parcel with one of our providers.
BookedA parcel is booked with a provider; label and tracking exist.
ShippedShipped on the channel with its own carrier; we only show status and tracking.
Cancelled · DraftOrder cancelled on the channel · manual shipment saved but not booked.
Tracking statusBooking · Failed · Label CreatedBeing booked · booking did not go through · label made, not yet scanned by the carrier.
In Transit · Out For Delivery · DeliveredMoving · on the last leg · delivered to the customer.
Exception · Carrier Lost · ReturnedCarrier reported a problem · carrier lost the parcel · coming back to us.
Void Pending · VoidedA cancel was requested and is in progress · the label is cancelled.
Duties (Incoterm)DDP DAP DDUDDP: we pay, added to the rate · DAP: customer pays on delivery · DDU: customer pays duties.
HandoverDrop-off PickupWe take it to the carrier · the carrier collects it.
