Procurement: choose a supplier, order, pack and invoice

From a price comparison to an approved Purchase Order, its packaging lists and its purchase invoice.

1 · Price AnalysisPick a product, enter several supplier rates, save. Approvers choose one supplier.
2 · Purchase OrderCreated for the chosen supplier, approved step by step (1st approver, then 2nd...).
3 · Payment requestOn approval, people on the Settings > Approvals Notification tab are told to raise a payment request in Finance.
4 · Packaging ListCreated against an approved PO, then attached to a Container.
5 · Purchase InvoiceCreated against a PO and approved before it is paid.

Who approves each step is set in Settings > Approvals (Price analysis Approve, PO Submit, Invoice Submit). A rejection goes back to the creator with a reason.

Procurement menu screenUse it to
ProductsKeep the products you buy; Price Analysis and Purchase Orders start from them.
Price AnalysisCompare supplier rates for one product and get one supplier approved.
Purchase OrdersOrder from a supplier; follow it from draft to received.
Packaging ListsRecord what is packed for a PO; assign it to a Container.
Purchase InvoicesRecord the supplier's invoice against a PO and send it for approval.
ContainersGroup packaging lists for shipping; covered in the Containers module.

Index · Procurement · Statuses

Procurement statuses

What each status means on Price Analysis, Purchase Orders, Purchase Invoices and Packaging Lists.

RecordStatusMeaning
Price analysisNO QUOTESCreated, no supplier rates entered yet.
AWAITING APPROVALRates saved and sent to approvers. A pending analysis can still be deleted.
APPROVEDAn approver accepted one supplier.
REJECTEDShown as a badge on a supplier, not as a list status.
Purchase orderDRAFT · PENDING FOR APPROVALBeing written · waiting for approvers in order.
APPROVEDApproved and locked. Set the production date to move on; an approver can Unlock for editing (saving locks it again).
IN PRODUCTION · PARTIALLY PACKED · FULLY PACKEDProduction date set; the ARD can now be entered · packing started · all units packed.
PARTIALLY SHIPPED · FULLY SHIPPED · RECEIVED · SHORT CLOSEDSome packing lists are in a container · every list is in a container · goods received at the warehouse · PO closed before every unit shipped.
Separate fields: payment (UNPAID / PARTIALLY PAID / PAID) and open or closed. READY_TO_SHIP is a legacy value.
Purchase invoiceDRAFT · PENDING FOR APPROVAL · REJECTEDBeing written · with approvers · sent back with a reason.
PAYMENT PENDING · PARTIALLY PAID · PAIDApproved and unpaid · some paid · fully paid.
GENERATING · READY · FAILEDPDF status: being built · ready to download · failed.
Packaging listAssigned · UnassignedNo status field; filtered by whether it is attached to a container. A PO can be packed once approved and open.
