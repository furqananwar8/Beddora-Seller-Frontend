Beddora · Shipping › Orders · Bulk labels, tracking & cancel5 / 5 · Rules and build map

Rules

ActionBehaviour
Entry pointOne "Bulk actions" menu in the selection bar. Each item shows n of selected it can act on; ineligible orders are skipped and listed with the reason before anything runs.
Print labelsNeeds a booking with a label (not channel-shipped, not voided). Same format choices as today's Print dialog, limited to formats all selected carriers offer; "Remember my choice" shared. PDF: one merged file in a new tab. ZPL: one combined .zpl download.
Commercial invoicesOnly cross-border parcels that have one; others skipped.
Refresh trackingPer parcel, a few at a time, with a progress count. Safe to repeat. Statuses already come from carrier scans; this just pulls them now.
Cancel / voidOnly unscanned labels. Shows the refund total, needs typing VOID from 5 labels up, voids each parcel on its own and reports Voided / Void pending / Failed. Failed rows can be retried alone.
PermissionsPrint and tracking need read; cancel needs shipping:orders write.

What exists, what's new

  • Reuse: PrintDialog shell (RadioCards, useLabelFormats, remembered format), usePrintDocument, CancelShipmentDialog wording and ConfirmDialog, useRefreshTrackingMutation, TrackingTimeline, STATUS_TONE, the Orders table and the selection bar from the bulk-booking design.
  • New UI: BulkPrintDialog, BulkCancelDialog, BulkTrackingDrawer, plus Tracking status and Carrier filters on Orders.

Backend needs

  • NEW Merged labels: the app has no PDF-merge or zip package, and the labels are fetched one by one today. Merging is best done on the server: POST /shipping/shipments/documents/bulk returns one PDF (or one ZPL).
  • HAVE Void and refresh tracking exist per shipment (/cancel, /tracking/refresh); looping them from the browser works for small batches. A bulk endpoint is better beyond about 20.
  • CHECK The orders list can't filter by tracking status or carrier yet.
  • CHECK What an order becomes after its label is voided (back to To Book so it can be rebooked, or Cancelled)? Not clear from the frontend.

Decisions for you

  • Merged PDF in one tab, or a downloaded file?
  • Threshold for typing VOID (5?), and a cap on bulk size (50?).
  • Is the tracking roll-up drawer needed in v1, or are the new filters and Refresh tracking enough?
