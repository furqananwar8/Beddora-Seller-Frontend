Supplier samples on purchase orders

A PO can now carry samples next to its products. Samples are tracked on their own, paid through their own payment request type, and shown per SKU in inventory as a count. They never touch packaging lists, containers, ordered units or stock.

1 · Purchase order

  • New Sample switch column in the Products table.
  • On a sample row, an empty price per unit means free; a price means chargeable.
  • Free: units only, price is locked. Chargeable: units and price are both required.
  • Everything else on the form is unchanged.

2 · Payment request

  • Paying against gets a fourth tab: Sample.
  • Same partner / PO / date / container / destination selection as the Purchase order tab.
  • User ticks which chargeable samples of that PO this request pays for.
  • Rest of the form (lines, GST, documents, approval) is unchanged.

3 · Inventory & Samples screen

  • A Samples column per SKU in warehouse inventory. Click the count to see its history.
  • New Samples screen: paginated list, search, filters applied only on Apply filters.
  • Samples have no physical effect on stock levels.

Sample lifecycle

Step 1
Added on PO
Free or chargeable, with units. Saved in the new po_samples table.
›
Step 2
PO approved
Samples are approved with the PO. Chargeable samples become Unpaid; free samples need no payment.
›
Step 3
Payment requested
Raised from the Sample tab. Sample shows Payment requested; a rejected request returns it to Unpaid.
›
Step 4
Payment done
Sample becomes Paid and is counted in the SKU's Samples column with the date.

Principle

Samples live in their own table, not as PO lines. Every existing flow (PO payments, packaging lists, containers, shipped units, ordered units, receiving, price analysis) reads po_order_lines only, so it keeps working untouched and a PO with 2 products + 1 sample behaves as a 2-product PO everywhere except the new sample surfaces. The same SKU can be both a product and a sample on one PO, which the existing unique key on PO lines would not allow.

1One Sample switch per row
  • Off: a normal product, exactly as today.
  • On: the row is a sample. Packaging columns show “—”: samples are never packed, shipped or counted in stock.
2Free or chargeable comes from the unit rate
  • Sample row with no unit rate (or 0): free. The rate box shows “Free”; units are still required.
  • Sample row with a unit rate: chargeable; amount = units × rate, payable from the Sample tab.
3Totals and rules
  • “Total” stays products only, so PO amount, left to pay and payment % are unchanged.
  • A separate sample line shows units and chargeable amount.
  • At least one row must be a product.
  • One form, saved to two tables behind the scenes.

Backend · Prisma

Data model

One new table, one join table, and a new payment reference type. No column is added to existing PO line tables.

po_samples NEW
idInt, PK
purchaseOrderIdFK → po_orders, cascade
productIdFK → po_products, restrict
unitsInt
isFreeBoolean
unitPriceDecimal(12,2); 0 when free
paymentStatusNOT_REQUIRED · UNPAID · REQUESTED · PAID
paidAtDateTime?, set when payment completes
inventoryAtDateTime?, when counted in inventory (the "added in inventory" date)
createdAt / updatedAt
@@unique([purchaseOrderId, productId]) · indexes on productId, paymentStatus, inventoryAt
payment_request_samples NEW
Same pattern as payment_request_shipments.
  • paymentRequestId → payment_requests, cascade
  • poSampleId → po_samples, restrict
  • Unique on poSampleId among open requests, enforced in the service.
Existing models, small additions
  • PaymentReferenceType gains SAMPLE; purchaseOrderId on the request is set for it too.
  • PoOrder gains the samples relation.
  • PO timeline events record sample add / change / paid.
Status transitions
Free → NOT_REQUIRED · Chargeable → UNPAID → REQUESTED → PAID
REQUESTED → UNPAID when the request is rejected or deleted
Chargeable samples appear in the SKU's Samples section when payment is completed in Payment process (inventoryAt = that date). Free samples appear when the PO is approved. They are a separate tally and are never added to on-hand units or any cost figure.

Delivery

Change map and open questions

Backend (bbeddora)

  • prisma: PoSample, PaymentRequestSample, SAMPLE reference type, migration.
  • procurement/purchase-orders: accept / validate a samples[] array on create and update, one sample service so rules live in one place.
  • finance/payment-requests: Sample reference handler (picker query, amount from samples, status moves on submit / reject / payment done) beside the existing invoice, PO and shipment handlers.
  • inventory: sample count per SKU in the warehouse report, sample history endpoint, paginated samples list endpoint.
  • permissions: new read permission for the Samples screen, create/edit follows the PO and payment permissions.

Frontend (fbeddora)

  • PoFormScreen: new PoSamplesSection reusing ProductPicker, NumericInput and form helpers; sample schema added to the zod PO schema (react-hook-form).
  • PaymentRequestFormScreen: fourth tab and SampleLinesPicker; the reference selectors are reused, not copied.
  • Warehouse inventory: a Samples figure column plus SampleHistoryModal.
  • Samples screen: new route using the shared table, pagination and applied-filters pattern.
  • Feedback through useApiFeedback, as elsewhere.

Decisions

3What does "payment done" mean?Payment completed in Payment process.Confirmed
4May a PO have samples only?No. A PO always has a product line; samples are flagged SKUs that do not belong to the PO's products.Confirmed
5Price analysis or landed cost?Never. Samples are excluded from every cost and unit figure.Confirmed
6Add samples to an already approved PO?Yes, through the existing unlock and approval rule.Confirmed
