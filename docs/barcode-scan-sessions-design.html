Bar Code: scan sessions

Every scan belongs to a session tied to a container, an FBA/WFS shipment or an FBM order, and is clearly Inventory in or Inventory out. Stock updates when the session ends, and UPCs we don't recognise are kept so they can be resolved later.

Nov 7, 2026Route /dashboard/barcodeStatus: awaiting approval

What changes

  1. Start session asks for the scenario: Container, FBA/WFS or FBM.
  2. The scenario sets the default type (in for containers, out for FBA/WFS and FBM).
  3. The user picks the container, shipment or order the session is for.
  4. On End session, scans are grouped by UPC and stock is added or deducted.
  5. Sessions are listed with a date range filter; unknown UPCs get their own list and can be resolved later.

Overview

Three scanning scenarios, one session flow.

Container

In by default

Receiving a container. Pick from containers in Inbound receiving with status Delivered at warehouse.

FBA/WFS

Out by default

Sending stock to Amazon FBA or Walmart WFS. Pick from shipments confirmed with Amazon or Walmart.

FBM

Out by default

Shipping a customer order from Amazon, Walmart or Temu. Pick from Shipping › Orders.

Session flow

Start session→Choose scenario→Type defaults (can change)→Select container, shipment or order→Confirm, type and source lock→Scan
→End session and confirm→Save session→Group by UPC→Match inventory→Add or deduct→Unmatched to resolve list

Resolve flow (later)

Unmatched UPCs→Link to product→Recount or keep original qty→Apply in or out from original session→UPC saved for future matches

Scanner guide

The existing guide moves into a modal opened from Guide. Setup steps are unchanged; scanning steps follow the session flow.

Scanner guide✕
How to scan
  1. Open this Bar Code page in Chrome on the scanner (Zebra TC57) and sign in.
  2. Tap Start session and choose Container, FBA/WFS or FBM. Check the inventory type.
  3. Select the container, shipment or order, then tap Start scanning.
  4. Check the badge reads "Scanner connected". If it says disconnected, tap Reconnect.
  5. Point the scanner at the barcode and press the yellow side trigger.
  6. Each scan appears at the top of the list. Tap a row to see everything it contains.
  7. When you're done, tap End session and confirm. Stock updates right away; unknown UPCs go to Unmatched UPCs.
One-time scanner setup (DataWedge)
  1. On the TC57, open the app drawer and open DataWedge.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right) → New profile. Name it "Beddora" and tap OK.
  3. Open the new profile and make sure "Profile enabled" is ticked.
  4. Tap Associated apps → ⋮ → New app/activity. Choose com.android.chrome, then choose * (all activities).
  5. Back in the profile, under Barcode input: turn Enabled on (1D, 2D and QR codes are on by default).
  6. Under Keystroke output: turn Enabled on. Then open Basic data formatting, turn Enable on and tick "Send ENTER key".
  7. Under Intent output: turn Enabled off.
  8. Close DataWedge, open Chrome, go to this page and scan any barcode to test.

Bar Code: scan sessionsBehavior and data

Behavior, data and open questions

What the system does, what it stores, and what needs a decision before build.

Sources per scenario

  • Container: Inbound receiving containers with status Delivered at warehouse. Default in.
  • FBA/WFS: shipments confirmed with Amazon or Walmart, not yet shipped. Default out.
  • FBM: orders in Shipping › Orders waiting to ship. Default out.

On End session

  1. Save the session with scenario, source, type, times, user and every scan.
  2. Group scans by UPC; match to inventory.
  3. Matched: add (in) or deduct (out) in one transaction.
  4. Record a result per UPC; unmatched go to Unmatched UPCs.

On Resolve

  1. Link to a product (optionally save the UPC).
  2. Use the original count or a recount.
  3. Apply with the original type; mark Resolved with who, when and qty.

Edge cases

  • Disconnect mid-session: scanning pauses; session stays open.
  • Accidental close: no ✕ while scanning; leaving the page asks first.
  • Refresh: scans saved as they arrive; resume the In progress session.
  • Zero scans: offer Discard instead of End session.
  • Two open sessions on the same container, shipment or order: warn before starting.
  • Double submit: End and Apply are idempotent.

Open questions

  1. Can Inventory out take stock below zero, or clamp and flag?
  2. Which inventory field holds the UPC?
  3. Should ending a session update the source (mark container received, shipment or order shipped)?
  4. Scanned more or less than expected: allow, warn or block?
  5. Who can resolve or ignore unmatched UPCs?
